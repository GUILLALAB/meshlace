Privacy policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Meshlace lets phones send end-to-end encrypted messages to each other over Bluetooth, without internet, SIM card or server. This page explains what that means for your data.

The short version

  • We run no servers. There is no account, no sign-up, no phone number and no email.
  • We collect nothing: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reports.
  • Release versions of the Android app cannot access the internet at all.
  • Your messages and contacts stay on your phone, encrypted.

What stays on your phone

  • Your identity: cryptographic keys created on your phone and the name you choose. The secret keys are kept in the Android Keystore and never leave the device.
  • Contacts and messages: stored in an encrypted database on your phone. They are not included in cloud backups.
  • A technical event log used to diagnose Bluetooth problems. It contains message identifiers and random device identifiers, never message text. It stays on your phone unless you choose to share it yourself from the debug screen.

What other phones receive

  • Your contacts receive the messages you send them, and delivery confirmations. Only they can decrypt them.
  • Nearby phones running Meshlace may carry your messages to reach a contact who is out of range. They only see encrypted data and short random addresses that change every few hours. They cannot read the content, see who wrote it, or identify the recipient. Carried messages are deleted when they expire (24 hours by default).
  • Likewise, your phone may carry encrypted messages for other people, within strict storage limits. You can turn the mesh off at any time.
  • When you pair by QR code, your name and public keys are shared with the person who scans it.

Permissions and why we need them

  • Bluetooth (scan, connect, advertise): to find nearby phones and exchange encrypted messages. Scan results are not used to determine your location.
  • Location (Android 11 and older only): Android requires it for Bluetooth scanning on these versions. Meshlace does not use or store your location.
  • Camera: only to scan a contact's QR code. Nothing is recorded or stored.
  • Notifications: to show that the mesh is running and that a new message arrived. Notifications never show the sender or the text.
  • Foreground service: to keep relaying messages while the app is in the background.

What we can't protect against

People nearby with Bluetooth equipment can notice that a phone is running Meshlace and see the timing and size of transmissions, though not their content. Someone with access to your unlocked phone can read your messages. A contact can copy or show messages you sent them. Delivery is not guaranteed if no phone carries the message in time.

Children

Meshlace is not directed at children under 16.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes your identity, contacts and messages from your phone. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover them, and we have nothing to delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
